Moving faster than you imagine and staying reliable

Tusker logistics is a brand that was created in 2018 with a simple and ambitious goal, to provide reliable and fast solutions to anyone needing to move their products from one place to another.

Search for the right brand that can keep up

Tusker logistics was growing and needed a brand that can match that growth. They needed a clear and bold way to express their mission and capabilities to clients with an innovative, energizing design.

Starting the heavy work

As part of the initial brand strategy work, I conducted an in-depth interview with the client to find out what they think their brand is how they wanted to communicate it. I also conducted my own research on who they serve and who their competitors are and their activities.

From that foundation, I did a design process that explored multiple concepts that reflected their brand pillars, innovation, reliability, expertise, and friendship. I eventually landed on a simple, energetic design language that helped position Tusker as a logistics company challenging the status quo.
