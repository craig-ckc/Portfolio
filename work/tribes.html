Connecting the family of Tribes on the cloud

All Races Tribes is a sports academy that caters to all ages, races, and cultures. It sees sport, handball in particular, as a way to nature, groom, and develop the young to become the great people of tomorrow. The academy takes sport as a weapon to save the young generation from poverty, child marriages, drug abuse, early marriages, or we can say any problem which harms people.

Coming together

The main goal was to provide a space where Tribes members will connect and feel welcome. The look, feel and engagement had to all come together to really set needed to have a welcoming mood and submerge the user in the app’s special atmosphere.

The indication of people taking part in challenges and talking about the club posts makes the app feel more engaging. The soft design paired with the Tribes colors creates an environment where its members can relax.
